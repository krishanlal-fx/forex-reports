Glossary: plain-English meaning of terms used in this report
5 day
The change from the close five trading sessions ago to the latest price.
Day
The change versus the previous daily close.
Range bar
A bar from the low to the high of a period; the marker shows where the latest price sits.
Stale
The market is shut, so the price shown is the last close, not a live quote.
If/then scenario
What could happen if a condition is met. Not a forecast and not advice to trade.
AEDT
Australian Eastern Daylight Time, Sydney time, UTC+11.
bp
Basis point, one hundredth of a percentage point (0.01%).
$/MWh
Australian dollars per megawatt hour, the unit of wholesale power prices.
Negative price
Generators pay to keep running, usually when rooftop solar floods the grid at midday.
NEM
National Electricity Market, the wholesale power grid across QLD, NSW, VIC, SA and TAS.
ETF
Exchange traded fund, a basket of shares that trades like one stock.
Futures
A contract for a set price at a future date, used as the main live price for oil, gas and metals.
Proxy
A related, freely available price used in place of one we cannot get.
Yield
The interest a bond pays as a percentage of its price.
